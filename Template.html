Your focused wellbeing conversation

Having the opportunity to talk about your personal wellbeing forms a meaningful foundation for all great conversations including your reflect and shape conversation and 121s that you have with your manager. When you're feeling your best (both physically and emotionally) we know you'll be able to thrive and GROW at the NHSBSA, helping us to fulfil our aspirations to be a great place to work.

Personal wellbeing has a huge impact on how we perform at work so as part of the NHS People Promise, we're offering all colleagues a focused wellbeing conversation. This is so you can understand the support and guidance that's available to you as an NHSBSA colleague and your manager can understand how best to support you.

Before you attend your focused wellbeing conversation, we would like you to reflect on your wellbeing by completing this template and sharing it with your manager. Together you will discuss and identify any actions to enable you to thrive and be your best self.
